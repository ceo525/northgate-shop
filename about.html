About

A small studio for geometric sky art.

Northgate Digital makes quiet wall art from circles, arcs and rings: moon phases, orbits, eclipses and starfields.

How the art is made

Each design is drawn in code from a short list of shapes and one fixed seed. That keeps the lines exact at any size, and it means a print can be reproduced precisely. There are no stock images and no filters.

How an order works

Choose a print and pay through Stripe's secure checkout. Your print is then made to order by a print partner and shipped to the address you give. Prints ship within the United States only, and the price you see already includes shipping.

What you receive

An unframed print at the size shown on its page. Frames in the pictures are there to show scale and are not included.
