NP-03 The Hinge. 21 December 2026, 20:50 UTC, Sun over the Tropic of Capricorn

The Sun stands over the Tropic of Capricorn, the southernmost point it reaches, and turns back. The shortest day in the north and the longest in the south happen at the same instant. Porphyry put the gate of return in Capricorn. Above the line, day. Below it, night. The hinge between them.

Source: US Naval Observatory, Earth's seasons 2026; Porphyry, On the Cave of the Nymphs.

The sheet is lettered SOLSTICE. A circle cut by the horizon, day above, night below, on cream.
