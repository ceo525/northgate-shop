One lap around the Sun is one year, about 940 million kilometres. By 30 you have gone round thirty times: roughly 28 billion kilometres. You were mostly sitting down.

The plate counts the laps. It does not pretend to be the Solar System. Seven orbits are drawn. There are eight planets. It is a diagram, not a scale model.

The sheet is lettered ORBIT NO. 1. Rust ellipses on a warm sand ground.
