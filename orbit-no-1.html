The card

NP-02 Revolutions. One revolution, 939.9 million km, 52 light-minutes

One revolution of the Sun is 939.9 million kilometres. Light needs 52 minutes to cover that distance. You do it in a year, at about 30 kilometres every second, mostly sitting down. At forty you have travelled about 37.6 billion kilometres. This is a diagram, not a map: seven orbits are drawn; there are eight planets.

Source: Wolfram|Alpha, Earth orbit circumference, 9.399 × 10^8 km.

The sheet is lettered ORBIT NO. 1. Rust ellipses on a warm sand ground.
