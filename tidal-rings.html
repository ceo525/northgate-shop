One point. Then a year. Then another. Tides are the Moon pulling on the sea from about 384,400 km away. Everything spreads from a single point, and each ring is one year of it.

Hang it for a 30th, a 40th, a 50th, or the year you met. Count the rings out loud. That is the use.

The sheet is lettered TIDAL RINGS. Pale contours on a deep teal ground, one dot at the centre.
