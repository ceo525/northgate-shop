The card

NP-06 Rings. Lunar laser ranging, Apollo reflectors, 3.8 cm a year

Ocean tides tug at the Moon's orbit. Laser beams bounced off mirrors the Apollo crews left on the surface show it moving away from Earth by 3.8 centimetres a year. Each ring is one year. Forty rings: about a metre and a half.

Source: NASA GSFC, lunar laser ranging; 40 × 3.8 cm = 152 cm.

The sheet is lettered TIDAL RINGS. Pale contours on a deep teal ground, one dot at the centre.

When someone asks

The tides push the Moon 3.8 centimetres farther away every year. By forty, it has moved about the height of the person you are.
