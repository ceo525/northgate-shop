The card

NP-05 Réseau. Carte du Ciel, Paris, 1887, more than 22,000 plates

Réseau: the grid printed onto the glass plates of the Carte du Ciel, begun in Paris in 1887, so every star could be measured against it. Twenty observatories exposed more than 22,000 plates. The map was never finished. The grid is how you prove where something was. This one has one star on it.

Source: Wikipedia, Carte du Ciel; Science Museum Group, réseau grid.

The sheet is lettered STAR GRID. A field of dots, a few brighter stars, one ringed gold-coloured star.

When someone asks

Astronomers photographed every star three times, in a little triangle, so they could tell a star from a speck of dust. A real star leaves three marks.
