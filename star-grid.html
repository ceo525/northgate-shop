Star Grid

In 1887 the Paris Observatory began the Carte du Ciel. Glass plates. A grid burned into each one, so a star could be measured, not just admired.

The grid is how you prove where something was. This plate keeps the grid, and one star set apart on it.

The sheet is lettered STAR GRID. A field of dots, a few brighter stars, one ringed gold star.

When someone asks

It is a measuring grid. One star does not sit with the others.
